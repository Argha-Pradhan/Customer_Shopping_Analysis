# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project exploring customer purchasing patterns, product preferences, sales performance, and spending behavior using Python, MySQL, and Power BI.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping data to understand purchasing behavior and identify patterns across product categories, customer demographics, payment methods, discounts, and subscription status.

The workflow includes data cleaning and transformation using Python, SQL-based analysis in MySQL, and interactive dashboard development in Power BI.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python:** Data cleaning and preprocessing
- **Pandas:** Data manipulation and transformation
- **MySQL:** SQL queries and business analysis
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and data visualization
- **Excel / CSV:** Processed data storage

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Transformation

Used Python and Pandas to prepare the dataset for analysis.

- Standardized column names.
- Handled missing review ratings using category-wise average imputation.
- Identified and removed a redundant column.
- Transformed purchase frequency into a numerical representation.
- Exported the cleaned dataset into CSV and Excel formats.

### 2. Answering Business Related questions with SQL Analysis

Used MySQL to investigate customer behavior and shopping trends through 16 analytical questions.

**Customer Analysis**
- Customer distribution by gender.
- Category purchasing preferences by gender.
- Average spending by subscription status.
- Customer purchase history.

**Product & Category Analysis**
- Most purchased product categories.
- Top 10 purchased products.
- Top-rated products.
- Average customer ratings by category.
- Category-wise sales performance.

**Sales & Purchasing Behavior**
- Top revenue-generating products.
- Revenue distribution by payment method.
- Discount usage and average purchase amount.
- Preferred shipping methods.
- Top purchasing locations.

### 3. Power BI Dashboard

Developed an dashboard to present key findings and summarize customer shopping behavior.

The dashboard covers:

- Key performance indicators: Total Customers, Total Sales, Average Purchase Amount, and Average Rating.
- Product category performance across genders.
- Purchase behavior across customer ages.
- Subscription status distribution.
- Seasonal purchasing patterns.
- Purchasing frequency across categories.
- Sales and customer rating comparisons.

## Key Objectives

- Understand customer purchasing patterns and product preferences.
- Examine spending differences across customer segments.
- Analyze the relationship between discounts and purchase amounts.
- Identify popular product categories and revenue-generating products.
- Present business insights through an interactive dashboard.
